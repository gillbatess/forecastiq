Everything a planner needs in one workspace

From a single department to a quarter-long portfolio view, ForecastIQ turns your sales history into decisions you can act on.

Automatic economic context

Pick a store and a week. Temperature, fuel price, CPI, unemployment, promotions and holiday flags are looked up for you.

Ranges, not just numbers

Every forecast comes with an 80% prediction range that widens honestly as you look further ahead.

Explainable drivers

See which factors pushed a forecast up or down, in dollars, so the number is easy to defend in a planning meeting.

Bulk forecasting

Upload a CSV of stores, departments and weeks. Get back a forecast for every row in seconds, ready to download.

API-first

Everything in the app is available through a documented REST API, so forecasts can flow into your own tools.

Honest accuracy

We report out-of-sample results only, measured against simple baselines, so you know what to expect.
